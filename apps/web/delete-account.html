Delete Your EazInvoice Account

You can request deletion of your EazInvoice account and associated personal access data. We verify that the request comes from the registered account owner before processing it.

On this page Request deletion Data removed or anonymized Data that may be retained What happens next

Request account deletion

Send an email from the registered email address for your EazInvoice account. Use the subject EazInvoice account deletion request and include the business name associated with the account.

Request Account Deletion

We may ask for additional confirmation before acting on a request. This email request does not delete an account merely by opening this page.

Data removed or anonymized

Where no retention requirement applies, we remove or disable personal account access information and may anonymize non-required personal metadata, preferences and account contact details.

Data that may be retained

Certain financial, tax, accounting, security, payment, audit or legal records may be retained where necessary to preserve statutory, audit, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or record-keeping obligations. Finalized business records may not be deleted if doing so would compromise accounting or statutory history.

Retention and access decisions are reviewed against the request and the applicable business records. EazInvoice does not claim that every record can be immediately erased.

What happens next

Support will verify account ownership, review any business-owner or record-retention constraints, disable access where appropriate, and explain any records that must remain retained. No fixed processing period is promised here because timing depends on the account and records involved.
